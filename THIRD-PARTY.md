this file exists so that fact is stated rather than buried.

## The Middlebury 2014 Stereo Dataset

**Where it appears**: `docs/panels/motorcycle_real.png`, the figure at the top
of the README. Its leftmost image is a frame from the dataset's "Motorcycle"
scene, shown to illustrate what the accuracy measurement was made against.

> D. Scharstein, H. Hirschmüller, Y. Kitajima, G. Krathwohl, N. Nešić,
> X. Wang, and P. Westling, *High-resolution stereo datasets with
> subpixel-accurate ground truth*, GCPR 2014.
> https://vision.middlebury.edu/stereo/data/scenes2014/

That frame belongs to its authors under their terms. The 0BSD grant does not
extend to it.

**The dataset is not redistributed here.** `data/real/` is untracked by design.
To reproduce the real-imagery results you fetch the scene yourself:

```sh
./scripts/fetch_middlebury.sh Motorcycle data/real
./scripts/prep_real.py data/real/Motorcycle-perfect data/real 3
```

which means every user accepts the dataset's terms directly from the people who
set them, rather than inheriting a copy through this repository.

## What the scene is used for

The accuracy reference, and only that. The **synthetic** scenes carry all
correctness gating — they can exercise the top of the disparity range, which
this scene cannot (its true disparities stop at 119.8). Every timing in the
Configuration A, B and C tables comes from synthetic scenes generated by code
in this repository. Delete the one figure and nothing stops building, running
or gating.

## Everything else

There is no other third-party material. Every implementation here — including
the Hexagon HVX kernels under `hvx/` and the column-major experiment under
`colmajor/` — is the author's own work and sits inside the 0BSD grant. No
separate terms attach to any of it.
